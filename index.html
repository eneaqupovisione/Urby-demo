01 — The houseEst. 2025

A bakery counterthat grew a roof.

URBY started with four stools, a record player and a sourdough starter someone carried over from Chiang Mai. It grew into a house: rooms above, a kitchen below, a courtyard where guests and neighbours end up on the same bench without planning to.

We cook what the morning market gives us, bake everything in house, and keep the lights low enough that nobody checks the time. Stay a night or stay for a coffee — the welcome is identical.
